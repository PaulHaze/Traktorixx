# Traktorixx

A Mixxx skin that looks and feels like the owner's Traktor setup. Terms use Traktor's
names, since those are what the owner thinks in. Where Mixxx's name differs, it goes
under _Avoid_.

## Layout

**Header bar**:
The top strip with the wordmark and status items (CPU, MIDI, power, main level, full screen, settings).
_Avoid_: menubar, toolbar

**FX row**:
The strip under the header bar with FX unit 1, the master panel and FX unit 2.

**Master panel**:
The centre of the FX row, holding the master tempo, AUTO/MASTER and MAIN volume.
_Avoid_: master section, clock section

**Library**:
The track browser below the decks.
_Avoid_: browser

## Deck

**Deck**:
One of the two track players, A (left) or B (right), each with its track info, waveforms, transport and loop controls.
_Avoid_: channel (that's the mixer strip), player

**Scrolling waveform**:
The large waveform that moves past a fixed playhead as the track plays, showing beat grid and cue markers.
_Avoid_: main waveform, waveform display

**Overview**:
The small whole-track waveform under the scrolling waveform, with numbered hotcues. Clicking it jumps to that point in the track.
_Avoid_: overview waveform, mini waveform

**CUE**:
Press and hold at the cue point to play. Release to stop and return to the cue point. Pressing PLAY while CUE is held hands over to normal playback, which continues after CUE is released.
_Avoid_: cue preview

**CUP**:
Cue-and-play: pressing jumps to the cue point and holds there, and playback starts on release.
_Avoid_: cue_play, cue-gotoandplay

**MASTER (deck)**:
Makes this deck the tempo reference that SYNC follows.
_Avoid_: sync leader

**Beatgrid panel**:
The section under a deck's transport for fixing a track's beat grid and cues (grid nudge, BPM, TAP, MOVE/CUE/GRID). Used during track preparation, not while DJing.
_Avoid_: advanced panel, edit deck

## FX

**FX unit**:
One effect processor, assigned to channels with the mixer's FX 1/2 buttons. Traktorixx shows each unit in single-effect mode.
_Avoid_: effect rack, effect chain

**Single-effect mode**:
An FX unit showing one effect with D/W, on/off, three parameter knobs and three parameter buttons.
_Avoid_: group mode (Traktor's three-effect mode, not used)
